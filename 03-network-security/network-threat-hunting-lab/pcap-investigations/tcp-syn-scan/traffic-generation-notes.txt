Scenario: Final TCP SYN Scan
Source: 192.168.10.10 (Windows USER)
Primary target/network: 192.168.20.10 or controlled DMZ addresses
Authorization: isolated VirtualBox lab owned and controlled by the project author

Method:
A controlled TCP SYN scan targeted ports 1–100 on `192.168.20.10`. The capture filter retained TCP control traffic for focused analysis.

Safety constraints:
- No public or third-party target was scanned.
- No production credentials were used.
- The activity was stopped after the evidence dataset was captured.
- Exact command screenshots are retained in the scenario evidence folder where applicable.

# ATT&CK and Control Mapping — P4-AUTH-01

A small controlled series of wrong-password attempts against a known temporary account aligns most precisely with **T1110.001 — Password Guessing**. The test does not claim large-scale brute force, credential stuffing, or password spraying.

- Which command, process, protocol, or control generated the evidence?
- Is the mapping reported by the security tool, added by the analyst, or both?

- Are legitimate administrative explanations documented?
